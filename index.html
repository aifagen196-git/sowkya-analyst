About me

I take complex business data and turn it into analysis, dashboards and forecasts that people actually use to make decisions.

Over five years across Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase and Accenture, I've built analytical models, executive dashboards, forecasting workflows and automated reporting for finance and operations teams.

Most of my work starts with a business requirement and ends with a reliable, repeatable answer — whether that's a Snowflake query, a Power BI report or a Python pipeline that removes a week of manual Excel work. I hold an MS in Data Science from Sacred Heart University.
